Frequently Asked Questions


You can track your order by visiting the "Returns & Orders" section in your account dashboard. Click on the specific order to view its real-time shipping status.

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on most items. If you are not satisfied, initiate a return from your Orders page and we will process your refund within 3-5 business days of receiving the item.

Orders can be cancelled before they ship. Go to Orders, select the item, and click 'Cancel'. If it has already shipped, please use the Returns process instead.

If your card was declined, please ensure your billing address matches exactly. We accept all major credit cards, UPI, and net banking.

Use the form on this page to send us a message, or call us at 1-800-SHOPHUB (available 24/7).
